Swapp

An Android app about time spent on social media.

Happy to show on request

Why

It's easy not to notice how much time goes into social apps. A number of minutes doesn't say much, but a walk or a chapter of a book does.

Swapp shows the time as something you recognise, and it does so without anything about you leaving the phone.

See exactly what the app reads and stores in the privacy policy.

How it works

  1. Counts the time you've spent in social apps during the day.
  2. Translates it into what the same time could have been, like a walk or a book chapter.
  3. Daily goals for time in social apps.
  4. A gentle pause screen as a reminder.
  5. A widget on the home screen.
  6. No internet permission at all. Nothing can leave the phone.

Built with

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Android
