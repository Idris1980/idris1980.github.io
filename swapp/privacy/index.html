Swapp Privacy Policy

Effective from 13 November 2026 (version 0.4).

Swapp is built so that nothing about you ever leaves your phone. This page explains exactly what the app reads, what it stores and what it never does.

The short answer

  • Swapp has no permission to use the internet. It cannot send anything anywhere, even if it tried.
  • There is no account, no login, no ads and no analytics sent to me or anyone else.
  • Everything Swapp stores stays on your phone. Uninstall the app and it is all gone.

What Swapp reads

To measure your time, Swapp needs the Usage access permission, which you turn on yourself in the phone's settings. It lets the app see when apps are opened and closed.

Swapp only uses this:

  • When the social apps are opened and closed: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, YouTube, X, Threads, Reddit, Pinterest and LinkedIn.
  • When the screen turns on and off, to work out how long the phone has been used in total.
  • For a short while after you have chosen something else (10–30 minutes), Swapp checks whether a social app is opened again, so it can ask if you really want to. None of this is stored.

Events from every other app are discarded the moment they are read and are never stored. Swapp never reads what you do inside an app: no messages, photos, searches, contacts or locations.

What Swapp stores on your phone

  • Your list of things you would rather do.
  • Your settings: reminder, pause screen, daily goal, evening summary and which apps should count.
  • Which suggestions you tapped "I'll do it!" on.
  • Your chosen language.
  • Two numbers per finished day: time on social media and time with the screen on. These are needed for the weekly overview, because Android only keeps its own log for about a week.

Swapp is excluded from the phone's cloud backup and is not transferred when you move to a new phone. None of this ends up with Google or anyone else.

How long data is kept

Daily numbers and choices older than 90 days are deleted automatically. Under Settings → Privacy and data you can tap Delete all my data at any time. Everything is deleted immediately, and the reminder and evening summary are turned off.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Usage accessMeasure time in the social apps
NotificationsShow the reminder when you have been scrolling for a while
Foreground serviceCheck once a minute whether it is time for a reminder. Android requires it to be visible as a notification, "Swapp is keeping track".
Run at startupRestart the reminder and the evening summary after the phone reboots, if you have turned them on
Display over other apps (optional)Show the pause screen on top of the social app. This permission can only draw on the screen, it cannot read what is shown. Only used if you choose Pause screen.

The reminder, the pause screen and the evening summary are off by default. They only start if you turn them on yourself.

Share my week

When you tap Share my week, Swapp draws a picture on the phone and opens the phone's normal share menu. Swapp itself sends nothing: you choose whether and where to share the picture. The picture only contains what you can see on it (the week's time, the bars, what the time was enough for and how many times you chose something else).

Widget

The home screen widget shows the same numbers as the app. It gets them from the phone, not from the internet.

Children

Swapp does not collect any data at all, from children or adults.

Contact

Questions about Swapp and privacy: kontakt@idrisaltun.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Swapp will never start sending data anywhere without it being clearly stated here first and you choosing it yourself.
